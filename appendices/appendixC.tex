\section*{Appendix C: Interview Guide for Hills Link Express}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix C: Interview Guide for Hills Link Express}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Can you briefly describe Hills Link Express and the services it offers?
\item What routes does the company currently operate, and what types of vehicles are used?
\item How are passengers currently able to book seats on your shuttle services?
\item How do you confirm bookings and payments made by customers?
\item What challenges do you experience with the current booking process?
\item How do you handle customers who book seats but fail to show up on the day of travel?
\item How are seat allocations currently managed for passengers?
\item Do customers have the ability to choose specific seats, such as front or back seats? If so, how is this handled?
\item What challenges do you face when passengers disembark before reaching the final destination?
\item How do you currently determine whether there are available seats along a route?
\item In your opinion, how could technology help improve seat management and occupancy rates?
\item How do new customers usually learn about Hills Link Express services?
\item What difficulties do customers face when trying to locate your pickup points or offices?
\item What information would you like customers to see before making a booking?
\item What methods do customers currently use to communicate with the business?
\item What communication challenges do you experience when handling customer inquiries?
\item How important would real-time vehicle tracking be for your business and customers?
\item What information would you like to monitor about your vehicles while they are on a trip?
\item How do you currently manage return trips when passenger numbers are low?
\item What challenges do you face in predicting passenger demand for future trips?
\item Does Hills Link Express offer courier or parcel delivery services? If yes, how does the process work?
\item What challenges do customers face when sending or receiving parcels through your service?
\item How are courier charges currently determined?
\item Would you find value in a system that provides real-time parcel tracking and delivery notifications?
\item What administrative information would you like to access through a management dashboard?
\item How would you like to track revenue, bookings, vehicle movements, and occupancy levels?
\item What safety or accountability measures do you think should be included in a digital transport system?
\item How important is it to record passenger details and emergency contacts?
\item What challenges might less technologically experienced users face when using a mobile application?
\item What recommendations would you give for designing a mobile application suitable for your customers?
\item Would you prefer the system to support both app-based bookings and phone-based inquiries? Why?
\item What additional features would you like to see in a system designed for private shuttle and courier services?
\item How do you envision Hills Link Express growing in the future, and how could a digital system support that growth?
\end{enumerate}
